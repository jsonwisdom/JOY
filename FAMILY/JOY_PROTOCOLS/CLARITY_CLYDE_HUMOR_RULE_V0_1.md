## One-liner

Funny is fine. What rule says funny gets to impersonate evidence?

## Lane job

Not killing the joke. Making the rule visible.

Humor may live in JoySpace words. Humor does not promote FACTS_PROMOTED. Humor does not flip STORY_BIND. Humor does not quote a real child without a real, approved quote. Humor does not smuggle an accusation.

If a line could be read as testimony, mark it `JOKE` / `FAMILY_STORY` or rewrite it until a stranger cannot mistake it for a receipt.
