# Three Daughters Algorithm v0.1

**Repository:** JSONWisdom / JOY  
**Status:** DRAFT — FAMILY LEGACY DESIGN  
**Authority:** No automatic execution or commercial publication  
**Primary steward:** Jay Wisdom / Mr. Wisdom

## Purpose

Preserve the Wisdom Family Legacy for Jaycee, Brianna, and Heidee without flattening three different lives into one story.

The algorithm keeps memory, evidence, emotion, privacy, and future product potential separate but connected.

## Core Rule

> Three daughters means three independent branches, one shared family root, and no forced consensus.

## Family Root

```text
WISDOM FAMILY LEGACY
├── JAYCEE
├── BRIANNA
└── HEIDEE
```

Each daughter receives:

- an independent memory lane;
- an independent consent lane;
- an independent privacy setting;
- an independent inheritance and stewardship record;
- an independent right to disagree with a shared family narrative.

## Replay Sequence

```text
SOURCE MEMORY
→ DAUGHTER-SPECIFIC BRANCH
→ DATE / PLACE / PERSON CHECK
→ SUPPORTING RECORD SEARCH
→ ANOMALY LOG
→ PRIVACY REVIEW
→ FAMILY MEANING REVIEW
→ DERIVATIVE PRODUCT REVIEW
→ HUMAN AUTHORIZATION
→ VERSIONED RELEASE OR HOLD
```

## Three-Lane Evaluation

### Lane 1 — Jaycee

Preserve Jaycee's memories, relationship history, creative interests, corrections, and future decisions as her own branch.

### Lane 2 — Brianna

Preserve Brianna's memories, relationship history, creative interests, corrections, and future decisions as her own branch.

### Lane 3 — Heidee

Preserve Heidee's memories, relationship history, creative interests, corrections, and future decisions as her own branch.

## Shared-Family Merge Rule

A record may enter the shared family lane only when:

1. its source is identified;
2. conflicting accounts remain attached;
3. privacy settings permit sharing;
4. no daughter's account is silently overwritten;
5. commercial use has a separate approval decision.

## Marriage and Divorce Expansion

Marriage, separation, divorce, reconciliation, custody, contact changes, households, and financial transitions must be stored as separate events.

```text
MARRIAGE DOES NOT ERASE PRE-MARRIAGE HISTORY
DIVORCE DOES NOT ERASE THE MARRIAGE
RECONCILIATION DOES NOT ERASE THE SEPARATION
ONE PERSON'S MEMORY DOES NOT ERASE ANOTHER'S
```

## Mind-the-Gap Protocol

Missing information is recorded as missing, not reconstructed as fact.

For every gap:

```text
EXPECTED RECORD
→ RECORD FOUND OR NOT FOUND
→ MEMORY ACCOUNT
→ CONFLICTING ACCOUNT
→ POSSIBLE EXPLANATIONS
→ VERIFICATION NEEDED
→ CURRENT STATUS
```

## Anomaly Classes

- date conflict;
- location conflict;
- identity or name variation;
- missing document;
- later memory contradicting earlier memory;
- shared asset or authorship uncertainty;
- privacy conflict;
- commercialization conflict;
- consent unknown;
- AI-generated addition not found in the source record.

## AI Boundary

AI may:

- classify;
- summarize;
- compare accounts;
- identify gaps;
- generate draft indexes;
- propose derivative products.

AI may not:

- invent missing memories;
- decide which daughter is correct;
- grant consent;
- publish private family material;
- transfer ownership or authority;
- promote an artifact into commerce.

## Commercial Derivative Rule

A family artifact is not automatically a product.

```text
PROTECTED FAMILY SOURCE
→ REDACTED / DERIVED EDITION
→ DAUGHTER-SPECIFIC PRIVACY CHECK
→ FAMILY MEANING CHECK
→ MR. WISDOM AUTHORIZATION
→ AMAZON / AWS CANDIDATE
```

## Succession Principle

The legacy must remain understandable even if Jay is unavailable.

Every approved artifact should include:

- plain-language purpose;
- source location;
- version;
- authorship and contributor fields;
- privacy status;
- verification method;
- stewardship instructions;
- commercial-use status.

## Governing Invariant

> One family root. Three independent daughter branches. Memory preserved. Gaps exposed. Consent explicit. Commerce derived—not assumed.
