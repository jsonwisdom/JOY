## Flip the Stack: the learner comes first

HerStory is a **student-authored learning option**, not a biography written about JayCee, a statement on her behalf, or an enrollment record. The learner may choose a question, decline participation, keep her work private, or correct the proposed story. No one is assigned a personal role, identity, consent, school affiliation, or achievement by this document.

JOY welcomes → JayCee asks → Sister Syntax protects → ALMS suggests → source receipts support a bounded classroom replay → human learner decides whether to continue.

Students are not owned by repos, assistants, schools, governments, or family projects. There is no automatic cross-repository sync.

## JoySpace front door and Sister Syntax

- **BLOSSOM:** open a fictional classroom story.
- **DISCOVER:** identify a source, date, scope, and missing evidence.
- **CREATE:** draw one football play or a flower-themed learning card.
- **REPLAY:** show how someone else could check the same claim.
- **PROTECT / REFLECT / SHARE-WHEN-SAFE / REST:** participation and publication are optional. No child names, addresses, schools, photos, medical information, messages, wallet credentials, or private family content enter a public receipt.

Three Sisters are teaching functions: **PRESERVE** original material, **CONTINUE** joyful learning without coercion, **ROUTE** to the next safe question. They create no consent or authority.

ALMS is **Acknowledge → Locate → Membrane-check → Suggest**. The librarian offers a shelf, not a verdict.

## Alabama boundary lesson — Iron Bowl classroom only

- **WAR EAGLE:** challenge an evidentiary claim and ask for the original.
- **ROLL TIDE:** defend a continuity plan and show its assumptions.
- **MAGNOLIANS:** preserve the original before interpretation.
- **DAISYS:** check details, accessibility, and missing steps.
- **APPLE BLOSSOMS:** teach back what is known, unknown, and simulated.

No real football team's affiliation, university adoption, state agency role, student enrollment, or academic approval is claimed. Team choice does not rank learners or establish historical truth.

### Three separations every student can replay

1. FAMILY RELATIONSHIP ≠ STUDENT CONSENT ≠ UNIVERSITY POLICY.
2. UNIVERSITY LOCATION IN ALABAMA ≠ STATE GOVERNMENT AUTHORITY.
3. GAME SCORE ≠ EVIDENCE VERDICT; GIT HASH ≠ SOURCE AUTHENTICITY.

A simulated cyber zero-day is **not** JOY's Ground Zero Day retrieval-debug method. A classroom response plan does not test or operate emergency systems. Real emergencies: call 911.

## OODA trial — the 168-case design, not a completed trial

The proposed educational cross product is:

`7 modeled divisions × 3 flower squads × 4 OODA phases × 2 online/offline modes = 168 unique design cases`.

**OBSERVE** choose a public source and a precise question.  
**ORIENT** check version, provenance, privacy, competing explanations.  
**DECIDE** select one bounded, reversible educational next step.  
**ACT** perform only an offline demonstration, record its result, and return choice to the learner.

No actual AEMA division, emergency agency, or public infrastructure is tasked or tested. No learning trial is reported completed by this document.

### Trial 001 — Hail Mary, gravity, and an original receipt

Use a *hypothetical*, non-weapon projectile model to calculate football flight; list assumptions (level landing, launch speed, angle, no drag). Separately inspect the linked football play's official published report. Record which statements are physics-model outputs, which are historical evidence, and which remain UNKNOWN. An animation, game metaphor, or photograph never replaces a source record.

## CI-FIRST acceptance checks

- **PASS** only for a narrowly scoped claim reproducible from original or identified public evidence.
- **FAIL** for a defined contradiction or test failure with a visible receipt.
- **UNKNOWN** when evidence, consent, runtime status, or source access is missing.
- Always record source URL, version/date, content digest if fetched and verified, provenance, scope, and limitations; use `null` for uncomputed source digests.
- Never fabricate a classroom result, award a verified badge without replay, treat a Git blob as personal identity proof, or promote a family relation into legal/academic authority.
