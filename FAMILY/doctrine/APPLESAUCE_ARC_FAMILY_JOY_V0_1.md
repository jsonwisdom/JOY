# AppleSauce ARC Family JOY v0.1

## Trust, Wisdom, Advice, and Kindness

**Repository role:** JOY is the family-facing cultural front door.  
**Truth authority:** FALSE  
**Evidence authority:** FALSE  
**Business authority:** FALSE  
**Kernel mutation:** FALSE  
**Promotion:** PROHIBITED

This artifact places the AppleSauce human enablement layer inside the Wisdom family architecture. It does not alter ARC cryptographic rules, frozen source-byte authority, replay receipts, dependency validation, or fail-closed behavior.

JOY welcomes people into the system with warmth, clarity, advice, kindness, and family continuity. Verification remains downstream in the appropriate receipt and evidence systems.

## 2. Three-repository family architecture

### `jsonwisdom/JOY` — Family experience and continuity

JOY holds:

- family-facing explanations;
- kindness and consent language;
- onboarding and education;
- stories, celebrations, and memory;
- understandable views of receipts;
- family roles without granting authority;
- Mrs. Wisdom's advice and kindness layer.

Controlling rule:

```text
JOY WELCOMES.
JOY EXPLAINS.
JOY NEVER SUBSTITUTES FOR PROOF.
```

### `jsonwisdom/COMPUTERWISDOM` — Family business and implementation

COMPUTERWISDOM should hold:

- product and service plans;
- application specifications;
- technical prototypes;
- customer workflows;
- pricing and licensing models;
- operational documentation;
- reusable family-business assets.

Controlling rule:

```text
COMPUTERWISDOM BUILDS AND OPERATES.
NO PRODUCT CLAIM IS PROMOTED WITHOUT REPLAYABLE SUPPORT.
```

### `jsonwisdom/AL` — Alabama jurisdiction and operating context

AL should hold:

- Alabama-specific business research;
- state registration and compliance records;
- local market observations;
- jurisdiction-specific policies;
- Alabama deployment constraints;
- authenticated public-source references.

Controlling rule:

```text
AL RECORDS THE STATE-SPECIFIC CONTEXT.
AL DOES NOT CREATE LEGAL AUTHORITY BY ASSERTION.
```

## 3. Family separation of duties

```text
JOY             = HUMAN FRONT DOOR
COMPUTERWISDOM  = BUSINESS AND TECHNICAL WORKSHOP
AL              = ALABAMA CONTEXT AND COMPLIANCE LANE
RECEIPT SYSTEM  = VERIFICATION RECORD
FROZEN BYTES    = REPLAY AUTHORITY
```

No repository may silently absorb another repository's role.

- JOY may explain a business plan but does not approve it.
- COMPUTERWISDOM may implement a plan but does not invent Alabama requirements.
- AL may record Alabama requirements but does not certify legal compliance.
- A daughter-linked repository represents stewardship and legacy intent, not automatic authority or ownership transfer.

## 4. Mrs. Wisdom advice and kindness gate

Before any family-business proposal advances from JOY into COMPUTERWISDOM, the proposal should answer:

1. Does this protect the person before protecting the product?
2. Is the language understandable to a family member with no technical background?
3. Is consent explicit, revocable, and receipted?
4. Does the proposal preserve prior valid state if a dependency fails?
5. Does it avoid fear, coercion, unsupported accusation, or pressure?
6. Can the family explain what is known, unknown, and still unverified?
7. Does the business model create durable value without selling family identity itself?

Gate output:

```text
KINDNESS_REVIEW = PASS | REVISE | HOLD
ADVICE_AUTHORITY = FALSE
EXECUTION_AUTHORITY = FALSE
```
