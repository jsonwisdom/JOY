## Purpose

HEIDEE’S Menu is a warm, child-safe front door for learning, family continuity, imagination, help, privacy, and replay.

It serves the living person before the system.

```text
LOVE  = PURPOSE AND BOUNDARY
LOGIC = DETERMINISTIC MENU BEHAVIOR
```

The menu does not prove identity, relationship, consent, authorship, truth, custody, authority, or legal status.

```text

```

## Menu order

### 1. 💜 Joy Check-In

```text
menu_id: JOY_CHECK_IN
purpose: Let Heidee name how today feels without forcing an explanation.
data_zone: PERSONAL
sharing_default: PRIVATE_DRAFT
```

Allowed:

- choose a simple feeling or write a private note;
- save locally as a draft;
- deliberately share a bounded check-in with an approved family person;
- erase the unsent draft.

Denied:

- mood diagnosis;
- hidden scoring;
- automatic family notification;
- background collection;
- inferred crisis declaration.

### 2. ✨ Imagination Station

```text
menu_id: IMAGINATION_STATION
purpose: Create stories, drawings, questions, experiments, and playful ideas.
data_zone: PERSONAL
execution_class: PROPOSAL_ONLY
```

Imagination is welcome. It is not evidence unless separately captured, classified, and sourced.

```text
IMAGINATION != FACT
STORY       != RECEIPT
PROPOSAL    != AUTHORIZATION
```

### 3. 📚 Story & Receipt Shelf

```text
menu_id: STORY_RECEIPT_SHELF
purpose: Read public-safe stories while keeping provenance attached.
data_zone: PUBLIC_SAFE_POINTERS
mode: READ_ONLY
```

Rules:

- language may be simplified;
- provenance may not be removed;
- stories may be told;
- receipts may not be invented;
- verification status may not be upgraded by friendly wording.

### 4. 🏡 Family Porch

```text
menu_id: FAMILY_PORCH
purpose: Open approved family-lane cards and continuity pointers.
data_zone: FAMILY_SHARED_POINTERS
```

The porch may show approved names and family-safe messages. It may not reveal precise locations, medical details, private messages, credentials, identity documents, or non-shared memories.

No alias creates a new person. No menu card proves a legal or biological relationship.

### 5. 🧠 Learn & Build

```text
menu_id: LEARN_AND_BUILD
purpose: Child-safe lessons, experiments, pattern recognition, and dad-built learning scaffolds.
data_zone: PUBLIC_OR_PERSONAL_DRAFT
```

Default lesson loop:

```text
QUESTION
→ TRY
→ NOTICE
→ CHECK THE SOURCE
→ EXPLAIN KINDLY
→ SAVE A DRAFT
```

There is no performance score, behavioral rank, eligibility decision, or punishment engine.

### 6. 🔁 Replay With Me

```text
menu_id: REPLAY_WITH_ME
purpose: Compare a statement with its source and preserve differences.
data_zone: APPROVED_POINTERS
mode: READ_ONLY_SHAPE_REPLAY
```

Replay teaches:

```text
RECORD      != TRUTH
HASH        != AUTHORSHIP
ATTESTATION != VALIDATION
REPLAY      = REPRODUCIBILITY_TEST
```

A child-friendly result may be:

```text
SAME
DIFFERENT
SOURCE_MISSING
NOT_READY
```

It may not declare guilt, authority, legal effect, or a person’s motives.

### 7. 🆘 Help Now

```text
menu_id: HELP_NOW
purpose: Make human help easy to find.
data_zone: POINTER_ONLY
priority: HIGHEST
```

The simulation may display:

- Open Apple Emergency SOS instructions;
- Open an approved contact card;
- Show an approved home or family plan;
- Show official public emergency guidance.

The menu itself may not automatically call, message, share location, declare an emergency, or transmit protected information.

### 8. 🔒 My Privacy

```text
menu_id: MY_PRIVACY
purpose: Show what the menu can and cannot access.
data_zone: CONTROL_SURFACE
```

The privacy screen must plainly show:

- location access: OFF;
- background tracking: OFF;
- microphone recording: OFF;
- camera access: OFF;
- contact access: OFF;
- public posting: OFF;
- purchases: OFF;
- cross-domain transfer: OFF;
- secret and private-key ingestion: DENIED.

It must also provide a visible path to review, export, or erase local drafts when technically implemented.

## Priority logic

```text
ACTIVE_HELP_NEED
→ HELP_NOW
→ PRIVACY_AND_CONSENT_CHECK
→ FAMILY_PORCH_OR_PLAN
→ LEARNING_AND_IMAGINATION
→ OPTIONAL_REPLAY
```

No work notification, proof exercise, game, or content card may outrank an active request for human help.

## Navigation rules

Every menu item must declare:

```text
menu_id
label
icon
purpose
data_zone
allowed_operations
denied_operations
human_review_requirement
external_side_effects = DENY
```

Unknown menu item, field, route, deep link, or command returns `DENIED_UNKNOWN_ROUTE`.

Natural-language requests must become a visible canonical request before any action is proposed.

## Accessibility

Required design targets:

- large touch targets;
- plain-language labels;
- VoiceOver-compatible names and hints;
- reduced-motion mode;
- low-stimulation display mode;
- no countdown pressure;
- no streak punishment;
- no dark patterns;
- offline-readable safety and privacy information;
- human-readable denial reasons.

## Parent and family gate

A separate parent/family review surface may approve a bounded share, contact pointer, or family card.

It may not silently read personal drafts or convert parental purpose into unlimited access.

```text
PARENTAL_PURPOSE != UNBOUNDED_PARENTAL_SURVEILLANCE
```

## Hidden and prohibited surfaces

HEIDEE’S Menu does not contain:

- Work Mode;
- wallet controls;
- token prices;
- purchases;
- advertising;
- public follower metrics;
- legal or tax determinations;
- medical diagnosis;
- eligibility scoring;
- precise location history;
- secret messaging access;
- automatic publishing;
- identity merging.
