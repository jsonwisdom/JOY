## 6. LOVE, consent, LOGIC, and Replay

```text
LOVE       = HUMAN_PURPOSE_AND_CARE_CONSTRAINT
CONSENT    = EXPLICIT_SCOPE_BOUND_PERMISSION_STATE
LOGIC      = DETERMINISTIC_POLICY_VALIDATION

```

LOVE supplies purpose but not authority. Consent is recorded only at the scope actually granted and is never inferred. LOGIC checks a canonical request against allowed and denied operations. Replay compares visible claims and sources without declaring truth, guilt, motive, authorship, legal effect, or identity.

Allowed replay outcomes:

```text

DIFFERENT
SOURCE_MISSING
NOT_READY
DENIED
```

Any consequential action requires separate human review and non-model authorization.

## 10. Negative-path requirements

The binding must fail closed when:

- the requested route or field is unknown;
- a protected data zone would cross into Work;
- identity or family relationship is inferred;
- consent is absent, expired, ambiguous, or outside scope;
- a replay source is missing;
- a model requests direct execution;
- the homepage digest differs from its declared digest;
- the GitHub transport envelope is mistaken for the full binary package;
- a cloud pointer is treated as source authority;
- any operation would silently publish, purchase, call, message, track, or share location.
