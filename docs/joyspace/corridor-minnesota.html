Purple Corridor · Public Records

Minnesota Corridor

Investigate real public questions about school meals, public finance, student privacy, technology and local decisions. ISD 742 is a research example, not an accusation or an affiliation.

PROTECT → PRESERVE → EXPAND

A teacher or trusted adult guides participation. No wallet, real-money credits, public student profiles or stranger messaging required.

Five classroom stations

1. School Meals

Locate published eligibility, access and meal policies. Separate rights from payment software.

2. Public Money

Find a public budget, invoice or board record. Compare dates, amounts and scope.

3. Student Privacy

Find a public privacy notice; never collect or publish student records.

4. Vendor Chain

Ask who performs a service versus who actually has lawful decision-making authority.

5. Replay

Document your claim, source, counterevidence and revised result.

Try a research replay

A school payment system reports a fee. Does that establish a violation of meal access? What records would settle the question?

Nothing is sent to the School, stored online or published. Save your own work before leaving the page.
