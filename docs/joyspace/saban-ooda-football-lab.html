🏈 Coaches + Librarians + Mathematicians

Nick Saban's publicly documented Michigan State and Alabama coaching history connects the Big Ten and SEC. This is a family-safe math and evidence lab—not a licensed coaching clinic.

Three kinds of teaching authority

🏈 Coach

Frames the question, teaches decision-making, asks what practice could improve. Does not invent a historical play call or give a student's grade.

📚 Librarian

Finds dated public game books and transcripts, flags missing evidence, and protects rights and privacy. Suggests sources; never pronounces verdicts.

∑ Mathematician

Recomputes scores, margins, deltas and reversibility. A correct number does not prove why a coach made a decision.
