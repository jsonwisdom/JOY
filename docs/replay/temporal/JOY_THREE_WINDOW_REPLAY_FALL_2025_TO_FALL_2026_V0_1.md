
6. Explicit statement of what is unknown.

## Temporal Clyde Pincer

The Pincer must close independently for each time window.

```text
FALL_2025_PINCER = OPEN
TODAY_PINCER     = OPEN
FALL_2026_PINCER = NOT_RUN
```

A current artifact cannot retroactively close the fall 2025 rail. A future checkpoint cannot inherit today's workflow result.

```text
CURRENT_HASH != PAST_SOURCE_HASH
CURRENT_PASS != FUTURE_PASS
ONE_WINDOW_MATCH != CROSS_WINDOW_BINDING
```

## Cross-window binding rule

Cross-window continuity requires all of the following:

```text
SAME_ARTIFACT_SCOPE
SAME_CANONICALIZATION_RULE
SAME_HASH_ALGORITHM_AND_DOMAIN
DECLARED_VERSION_TRANSITIONS
NO_HIDDEN_BYTE_SUBSTITUTION
INDEPENDENT_REPRODUCTION_PER_WINDOW
EXPLICIT_VARIANCE_RECEIPTS
```

If an artifact legitimately changes, continuity is shown through a version transition and variance receipt—not by claiming identical roots.
