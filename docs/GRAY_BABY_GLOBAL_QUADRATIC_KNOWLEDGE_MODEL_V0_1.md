## Mission

Merge the Chinese, American, and UK Gray Baby story lanes into one replayable knowledge-management model without flattening their cultural differences or turning fiction into factual alien-disclosure claims.

Gray Baby is a fictional character and systems metaphor unless a separately identified artifact provides independently verifiable evidence.

## The Three Cultural Lanes

### China Lane — Mathematical Comedy and Family Logic

Primary language: Chinese.

Functions:

- uses absurd mathematical reasoning to challenge premature classification;
- centers family, neighbor, classroom, and domestic continuity;
- treats humor as a safe interface for difficult systems questions;
- separates observation, interpretation, and diagnosis;
- keeps the child protected from political, medical, or social inference.

Canonical motif: Gray Baby communicates through equations while adults repeatedly over-interpret them.

### American Lane — Evidence, Disclosure, and Institutional Replay

Primary language: English / American media and civic vocabulary.

Functions:

- reframes alien disclosure as an evidence-governance problem;
- distinguishes search absence from nonexistence;
- distinguishes domestic authority from truth;
- uses receipts, hashes, provenance, and archival replay;
- satirizes intelligence systems that convert ambiguity into action.

Canonical motif: the Gray Door permits synthetic testing but allows no unsupported conclusion to leave the corridor.

### UK Lane — Public-Safety Character and Global Education

Primary language: English / international classroom and public-information vocabulary.

Functions:

- turns Gray Baby into a globally understandable character;
- emphasizes child safety, consent, art, education, and design;
- uses evidence boards, BOLO parody, and character sheets without criminalizing the subject;
- makes institutional restraint legible to children and families;
- supports museum, classroom, broadcaster, and public-service adaptations.

Canonical motif: Gray Baby is a character of evidence, not assumptions.

## Quadratic Informational Model

The model is quadratic because every claim is evaluated across two dimensions, producing four operational quadrants.

```text
X AXIS: STORY <--------------------------> EVIDENCE
Y AXIS: PRIVATE/PROTECTED <-------------> PUBLIC/REPLAYABLE
```

| Quadrant | Name | Purpose | Allowed Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Protected Story | imagination, family meaning, private drafts | story without identity exposure |
| Q2 | Public Story | screenplay, art, satire, classroom character | clearly labeled fiction |
| Q3 | Protected Evidence | private source material, consent records, sensitive provenance | pointer-only or withheld |
| Q4 | Public Evidence | hashes, manifests, public receipts, synthetic test cases | independently replayable facts |

No artifact may move diagonally from Protected Story directly to Public Evidence. It must pass through explicit consent, source validation, and classification review.

## Quadratic Function

```text
GB(x, y) = aS² + bSE + cE²
```

Where:

- `S` = narrative or symbolic content;
- `E` = independently supportable evidence;
- `a` = cultural-context weight;
- `b` = translation and interpretation risk;
- `c` = verification strength.

The function is not a truth calculator. It is a routing model.

```text
if E == 0:
    output = FICTION_OR_UNVERIFIED

if S > 0 and E > 0:
    output = STORY_WITH_EVIDENCE_BOUNDARIES

if private_data == true and consent != verified:
    output = HOLD_PRIVATE

if claim == alien_disclosure and independent_evidence == absent:
    output = FICTIONAL_METAPHOR_ONLY
```

## Merge Rules

1. Preserve original-language artifacts alongside translations.
2. Never rewrite cultural differences into one generic voice.
3. Label fictional institutions, quotations, and events clearly.
4. Never present Gray Baby as real alien disclosure without independently verified evidence.
5. Child identity, family identity, medical claims, and private records remain protected.
6. A visual render is an artistic artifact, not photographic evidence.
7. Search failure does not prove nonexistence.
8. Repetition does not convert a claim into evidence.
9. Humor may challenge authority but cannot create authority.
10. Every public evidence claim must point to a replayable source or remain `UNVERIFIED`.
