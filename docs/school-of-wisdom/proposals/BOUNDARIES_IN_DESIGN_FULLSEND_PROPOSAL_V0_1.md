## 1. Big Idea

Build a kid-safe learning fabric where help is routed by need, not title; private thought stays private by default; every crossing declares what moves, who chooses, who receives it, why it is needed, what receipt is created, and how the learner returns to normal learning.

Core doctrine:

CAPABILITY ≠ PERMISSION
BOUNDARY ≠ HIERARCHY
BOUNDARY = DECLARED TRANSITION CONDITION
DOCTRINE = TEST
DOCTRINE ≠ ENFORCEMENT
DOCTRINE ≠ AUTHORITY

Kid version:

Know the line.
Ask before crossing it.
Share only what is needed.
Keep the receipt.
Replay what happened.

## 6. Return Path Model

FINDING_RETURN_PATH_001:
Existing objects define forward crossings more clearly than return crossings.

A return path must distinguish at least three event classes:

A. RESPONSE_RETURN
The destination answers and the answer returns to the learner.

B. UNACTIONED_RETURN
The destination cannot or does not act and returns the request unactioned.

C. RECALL_BEFORE_ACTION
The learner attempts to recall the outbound request before the destination acts.

These are candidate distinctions only. They are not yet locked rules.

Every return leg must answer the same crossing questions:
- who chooses
- what crosses back
- who receives it
- for what purpose
- what receipt exists
- what state is restored
- what cannot be undone

- What observable threshold can trigger emergency routing?
- Who or what may determine that threshold?
- What minimum information may cross?
- Which destination class is authorized?
- Must a teacher, parent, principal, or local emergency responder receive it?
- When is immediate action allowed without ordinary learner-selected routing?
- What receipt records the disclosure?
- What information must not cross?
- What is the post-event replay path?
- How does the learner return to normal learning state?
- What deletion, revocation, or access limitations apply after the event?

No answer is supplied by this proposal.

## 11. What Success Looks Like

"I don't understand."

"Do you want help finding it, learning it, or fixing a school process?"

The destination gets only what is needed.
The learner gets a receipt.
The learner can replay what happened.
The learner returns to learning.
