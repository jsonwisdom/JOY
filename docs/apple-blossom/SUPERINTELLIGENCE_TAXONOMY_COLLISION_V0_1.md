## Question

Does "super intelligence" already exist as a technical/research concept, and does that collide with the proposed naming labels?

## Answer

Yes, the research concept is already present in NIST-published material.

NIST AMS 100-75 summarizes a keynote taxonomy that includes "Artificial Super Intelligence." A separate NIST-published paper, "AI Security & Alignment Limitations," discusses hypothetical Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI) systems.

That does not make ASI a replacement federal name for AI.
