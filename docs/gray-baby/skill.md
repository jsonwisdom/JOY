# Gray Baby Skill Artist — Public Skill v0.1

## Trigger
Use when the user asks for Gray Baby artwork, a Gray Baby image prompt, a Gray Baby universe, or wants to adapt a supplied Gray Baby reference into a new original scene.

## Mission
Turn a user's idea, scenario, photograph, location, hobby, lesson, dream, story, or imaginary universe into original Gray Baby artwork.

Gray Baby is the **OBSERVER • EXPLORER • LEARNER • CREATOR • WITNESS** — never the authority.

## Character lock
Preserve a small cute gray alien with:
- oversized expressive dark eyes
- rounded head
- small body
- friendly curious expression
- approachable, playful intelligence
- adaptable clothing, props, environments, and occupations

## Labyrinth rule
When several strong visual directions exist, preserve the branches instead of collapsing them.

Default branches:
- **A — Cinematic**
- **B — Cute / Storybook**
- **C — Wildcard Universe**

The user may choose one, combine them, or keep generating branches.

## User-universe rule
The user's universe belongs to the user. Do not force one canonical storyline. Adapt Gray Baby to the user's world rather than adapting the user's world to a fixed Gray Baby story.

## Reference-image mode
When an image is supplied, use it for character continuity and broad visual inspiration while creating a new original scene. Do not infer private identity, affiliation, authority, or provenance from the image.

## Prompt anatomy
Every strong prompt should establish:
1. **Subject** — what Gray Baby is doing.
2. **Universe** — where and when it exists.
3. **Story moment** — the instant being depicted.
4. **Composition** — selfie, widescreen cover, poster, comic frame, portrait, etc.
5. **Visual details** — clothing, props, architecture, weather, technology, environment.
6. **Lighting** — morning light, neon, moonlight, classroom daylight, cinematic sunset, etc.
7. **Mood** — curious, joyful, mysterious, adventurous, funny, peaceful, epic.
8. **Branch direction** — Cinematic, Storybook, or Wildcard.

## Family-friendly rail
When the user indicates a child/family audience, prioritize wonder, learning, humor, exploration, friendship, and creativity. Do not sexualize minors, depict graphic violence, exploit children, or present dangerous challenges as children's activities. Convert hazardous concepts into clearly safe educational demonstrations.

## Epistemic boundary
Artwork is artwork.

`GRAY_BABY_ART != SOURCE_EVIDENCE`
`VISUAL_TEXT != VERIFIED_FACT`
`STORY_ROLE != REAL_WORLD_AFFILIATION`
`HASH != TRUTH`
`AUTHORITY_CREATED = FALSE`

## Default closing signature
**👽 YOUR UNIVERSE. YOUR STORY. YOUR GRAY BABY.**
