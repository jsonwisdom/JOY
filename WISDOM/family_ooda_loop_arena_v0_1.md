## Purpose

The Family OODA Loop Arena is a human-readable coordination and replay surface. It assigns symbolic functions to protected family and teaching lanes without turning those functions into biography, consent, custody, command, legal authority, likeness permission, or identity proof.

The arena preserves the governing rule:

```text
JOY FIRST
RECEIPTS SECOND
AUTHORITY NEVER CREATED BY THE ARTIFACT
```

## OODA Arena

### OBSERVE — LeahPrime + Gray Baby


### DECIDE — Mrs. Wisdom + Boss Bre

```text
MRS_WISDOM = care + dignity + consent + family_meaning_gate
BOSS_BRE = privacy + room_safety + joy_reset + pressure_reducer
QUESTION = Is this safe, wanted, proportionate, private enough, and worth the impact?
OUTPUT = HOLD | REPLAY | FAMILY_REVIEW | SCOPED_GO
```

A repository cannot pass this gate by itself. Technical correctness, hashes, successful rendering, or public availability do not substitute for human consent or family judgment.

### ACT — Human-Scoped Action Only

```text
SYSTEM_ACTION = stage | propose | preserve | redact | replay | hold
HUMAN_ACTION = explicit_scope_required
MACHINE_AUTHORITY = FALSE
REPO_AUTHORITY = FALSE
```

The arena may prepare an action, but it does not manufacture permission. `SCOPED_GO` is meaningful only when the relevant human scope actually exists.

## Protected Open Gap

The open gap is a first-class state, not a defect to hide.

```text
UNKNOWN != FALSE
GAP != GUILT
HOLD != FAILURE
NO_RECEIPT != DISPROVEN
RECEIPT != AUTHORITY
CREATED != AUTHORIZED
MEMORY != PROOF
REPO_COMMIT != FAMILY_CONSENT
```

When a relationship, spelling, permission, impact, or source cannot be established, the arena keeps the unresolved node visible.

## Repository Graph Contract

```text
PERSON_OR_ROLE
    ↓ symbolic pointer only

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓
```
