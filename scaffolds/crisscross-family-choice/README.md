## Algorithm law

An algorithm gives structure; it does not assign destiny.

Each daughter may:

- reject the slot;
- rename her surface;
- choose a different tool or repository;
- keep work private;
- collaborate selectively;
- publish one artifact without joining the whole system;
- correct family descriptions;
- leave without losing attribution to approved work.

Jason may offer the system as Dad and Builder. The system may not take a daughter's identity, voice, consent, labor, political position, privacy, or future.
