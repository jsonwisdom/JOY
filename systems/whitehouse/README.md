References to “White House” are scenario labels only. This module is not affiliated with, endorsed by, connected to, or acting on behalf of the White House, the Executive Office of the President, the United States government, any federal agency, or any government official.

## Purpose

The module supports hypothetical civic modeling, procedural scenario replay, semantic witness structure, bounded documentation, and receipt-based evidence logging without authority creation.

Receipts may record that an event was observed, simulated, replayed, or recorded. They may not claim that an event creates lawful executive power, official status, or binding governmental authority.
