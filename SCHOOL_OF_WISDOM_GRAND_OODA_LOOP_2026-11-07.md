# SCHOOL OF WISDOM — GRAND OODA LOOP

Date: 2026-11-07. Status: design/workflow proposal, not automated execution. Authority: false.

## Purpose
The School of Wisdom serves **students**. Technology, family projects, repositories, and bots are learning infrastructure, not owners of students. The loop returns to student agency, evidence, and improved understanding.

## Three-repository learning core
1. **COMPUTERWISDOM:** Legacy coordination, contract boundaries, provenance, and human oversight.
2. **AL:** Protection/boundary layer; its exact deployed enforcement is not established by this document.
3. **JOY:** Welcoming JoySpace patterns, family-safe learning, creativity, voluntary participation.

**HEIDEE** is an independent family implementation of JoySpace, not a subaccount or possession of JOY. Heidee supervises herself. Helpers support rather than replace her. Daddy-and-Me, J Space, and Sacred Mary D-Bot quad auditing are proposed audit roles; live operation remains UNKNOWN.

## OODA loop
### O — OBSERVE
Students choose a question and evidence to inspect. Collect primary-source links, dates, source provenance, and privacy/consent boundaries. Do not collect sensitive student information into public repositories.

### O — ORIENT
Compare the evidence against COMPUTERWISDOM coordination, AL boundaries, and JOY learning patterns. Distinguish verified facts from proposals, satire, and unknowns. Route family-specific JoySpace matters to HEIDEE only with appropriate consent.

### D — DECIDE
Students select an educational next action; human supervisors approve consequential actions. No bot can infer permission to publish family information, enter contracts, contact members, or deploy Workers from this file.

### A — ACT
Perform a small learning exercise: explain a claim, reproduce a calculation, audit a source, or propose a GitHub change through the required review workflow. Record PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN and the evidence supporting each result.

### RETURN TO STUDENTS
Ask what the student learned, what remains uncertain, and whether they wish to continue. Feed their feedback into the next OBSERVE step. Joy is optional; privacy and independent spaces persist.

## Nested loops
- **Student loop:** question → evidence → lesson → feedback.
- **Repository loop:** proposal → tests → protected-branch PR → review → merge → verify.
- **Family oversight loop:** bounded scope → independent audit → human decision → correction.
- **School-wide loop:** aggregate de-identified lessons and improve the curriculum without ranking people or asserting automated authority.

## CI-FIRST checks
- PASS: JOY, AL, and HEIDEE have public documentation of family coordination and boundaries.
- FAIL: Any attempt to bypass required repository protections or publish private family data.
- UNKNOWN: Actual bot ingestion, Cloudflare deployment, student enrollment, quad-audit execution, or consent beyond the stated design.
